Delete your StarTier account

StarTier is run by King Jordan Media LLC. You can delete your account at any time.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Tap the button below. It opens StarTier.
  2. Log in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.
Open StarTier to delete my account

In the StarTier app, you can also open your account (the circle with your initials, top right) and tap Delete my account at the bottom.

Or ask us

Email support@kingjordanapps.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my StarTier account". We reply within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, picture, bio and links
  • Your songs, videos, art, posts and comments
  • Your messages, follows, saves and playlists
  • Your paid plans and Galaxy memberships are cancelled right away, so you aren't charged again

What we keep, and for how long

  • Records of purchases and payouts that tax and financial laws require us to keep
  • If you are an artist who has sold something or set up payouts, we finish deleting within 30 days, so we can pay you what you're owed and fans keep what they bought
